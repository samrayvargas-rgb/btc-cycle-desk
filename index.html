Where spot sits — five rails

These are not buy/don’t-buy grades. They say how picky to be. Open = at or under that rail. Favorable = within 20% above. Selective = 20–40% above (add with a signal). Tactical = more than 40% above (location is not the reason).

Look ahead — power law and realized price

These lines only depend on Bitcoin’s age. Pick a date and you get floor / trend / ceiling. Realized price is left off — it does not follow a clean yearly rate.

Realized price — did this cycle tag it?

Realized price is a rough average of what holders paid. In prior cycles, spot has dropped to or under that line during the bear. This card asks whether that washout has happened yet.
